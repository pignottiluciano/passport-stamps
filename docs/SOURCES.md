# Catalog coverage and sources

Verification date: November 7, 2026.

## Country membership

Primary source: United Nations, [Member States](https://www.un.org/about-us/member-states).

The collection was checked against 193 member entries and country codes. `data/un-members.json` records the reproducible reference, official names, date, and source. Official names are retained in `officialName`; the gallery uses common English names.

The United Nations Dag Hammarskjöld Library also provides [current membership resources](https://research.un.org/en/unmembers/currentmembers).

The primary catalog excludes territories, observer states, and cities. Destinations belong to a separate catalog.

## Names, regions, and artwork

Country display names were prepared using the local environment's English `Intl.DisplayNames` data, then fixed in `data/stamps.json`. The build does not fetch names or update them when the environment changes. Artwork lettering may retain local-language forms. Aliases preserve alternative spellings, transliterations, and some earlier Spanish display names.

The five navigation regions and motif choices are editorial decisions. Natural, cultural, and architectural motifs are not necessarily official national symbols or unique to a country. The 193 country stamps use 129 motifs, some shared.

The UN is cited for membership facts, not as the creator or endorser of the artwork. No UN flags, logos, illustrations, or graphic files were reused. The MIT license covers this repository's code and original drawings.

## Destination landmarks and scope

The 238 destinations were selected with the project owner: 60 initial places and an expansion of 178 European cities. This is not an official tourism ranking.

Primary references consulted for names and associations include:

- [National Flag Memorial](https://www.monumentoalabandera.gob.ar/), Rosario.
- [Madrid tourism: Bear and Strawberry Tree](https://www.esmadrid.com/informacion-turistica/oso-madrono).
- [Spain.info: Córdoba](https://www.spain.info/es/destino/cordoba/), Mosque–Cathedral.
- [Notre-Dame de Fourvière](https://www.fourviere.org/fr/visiter-notre-dame-de-fourviere/), Lyon.
- [Marseille tourism: Notre-Dame de la Garde](https://www.marseille-tourisme.com/decouvrez-marseille/culture-et-patrimoine/sites-et-monuments/la-basilique-notre-dame-de-la-garde/).
- [Bavarian Palace Administration: Neuschwanstein](https://neuschwanstein.de/englisch/tourist/howtoget.htm), near Schwangau and Füssen.
- [Bologna Welcome: Two Towers](https://www.bolognawelcome.com/en/places/towers-historic-buildings/the-two-towers-garisenda-and-degli-asinelli-eng).
- [Italia.it: Alberobello and trulli](https://www.italia.it/en/puglia/things-to-do/alberobello).
- [Visit Portugal: Aveiro](https://www.visitportugal.com/en/destinos/aveiro/73753), moliceiro boats.
- [Visit Copenhagen: Nyhavn](https://www.visitcopenhagen.com/copenhagen/planning/nyhavn-gdk474735).
- [Visit Ljubljana: Dragon Bridge](https://www.visitljubljana.com/en/poi/dragon-bridge).
- [Slovenia.info: Ljubljana](https://www.slovenia.info/en/places-to-go/regions/ljubljana-central-slovenia/ljubljana).

These are factual references, not illustration sources. SVG silhouettes were drawn for this collection without downloading or tracing their photographs or graphics. They are simplified interpretations, not exact architectural reproductions. Some suitable motifs are reused; the European expansion introduced 124 additional motifs.

`associationNote` explains landmarks outside the city itself. Mont-Saint-Michel is classified as a landmark destination. Natural motifs may represent the wider tourist area. See the [destination catalog](DESTINATIONS.md) for individual motifs and notes.
