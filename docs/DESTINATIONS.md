## Surrounding-area references

- **Puerto Iguazú:** The falls are an attraction near Puerto Iguazú, in Iguazú National Park.
- **Puerto Madryn:** The whale represents the coastal corridor around Puerto Madryn and Península Valdés.
- **El Calafate:** Perito Moreno Glacier is part of the tourist surroundings of El Calafate, in Los Glaciares National Park.
- **Ushuaia:** The lighthouse represents the surroundings of the Beagle Channel.
- **Chamonix:** Mont Blanc represents the alpine surroundings of Chamonix.
- **Füssen:** Neuschwanstein is in Schwangau, near Füssen; it represents the wider tourist area.
